# Sharing TV Town with friends

Your friends need **only their phone and their TV**: no computer and no cables. It takes about ten
minutes. Their TV must be an Android TV or Google TV (see *Which TVs work* in [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)).

## What to send them

**Once the project is public** (the easiest way): just the download link. It always gives the newest version:

```
https://github.com/NareshJagriya/tv-town-launcher/releases/latest/download/TV-Town.apk
```

It's long to type on a TV remote, so paste it into a free link shortener such as tinyurl.com, and send the short link instead. Their TV then updates itself from Settings → Updates.

## The message to send (copy and paste)

> **TV Town: a new home screen for your Android TV** 📺
>
> **1. Install the free *Downloader* app on your TV** (search "Downloader" in the TV's Play Store; the orange icon by AFTVnews).
>
> **2. Open Downloader and type this link:** (put your short link here)
> (Or, if I sent you the file: install **Send files to TV** on your phone and on your TV, both free in the Play Store,
> and send TV-Town.apk from your phone to the TV.)
>
> **3. Install:** the TV asks to allow Downloader (or Send files to TV) to install apps. Choose **Settings**, switch
> it **on**, press **Back**, then **Install**.
> If **Play Protect** warns or says *Blocked*, choose **Install anyway**. It warns about every app from outside the Play Store.
>
> **4. Open TV Town** and follow the Welcome screen: your country and languages, then the two switches it asks for
> (each button takes you straight to the right screen of the TV's settings).
>
> **5. Optional:** for film and series information, TV Town shows a QR code: scan it with your phone and follow the
> steps (a free key, two minutes).
>
> If a switch is greyed out: TV Settings → Apps → TV Town → **Allow restricted settings**, then try again.
> To go back to the TV's own home: TV Town's Settings → *Switch to the TV's own home*.

## If they get stuck
- **"App not installed":** they already have a TV Town signed differently (e.g. a test copy). Uninstall it first.
- **Home goes to the TV's own home:** the *TV Town home helper* switch (TV Settings → Accessibility) is off. Turn it on.
- **No films or series on Home:** no TMDB key yet. Settings → *Films and series info*, then the QR code.
- **Fire TV, Samsung, LG, Roku:** TV Town can't be the home screen there (see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)).
