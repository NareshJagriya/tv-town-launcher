## Data and services

TV Town asks these services for information while you use it. Their content stays theirs.

- **TMDB** (films, series, posters): this product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. https://www.themoviedb.org
- **ESPN** (scores, scorecards, match stats, news, photos, team badges and league tables): from ESPN's public site feeds. ESPN content and team badges belong to ESPN and the teams. TV Town has no affiliation with ESPN. https://www.espn.com
- **OpenSubtitles** (subtitles, with your own free key): https://www.opensubtitles.com
- **LRCLIB** (song lyrics): https://lrclib.net
- **iTunes Search** (song names and covers): Apple. https://www.apple.com/itunes
- **Wikipedia and Wikidata** (artist photos and facts): each under its own licence on Wikimedia Commons; Wikidata under CC0.
- **Open-Meteo** (weather): CC BY 4.0, https://open-meteo.com
- **JustWatch** (where a title streams, and some posters): https://www.justwatch.com
- **StreamRadar India** (top 10s on Indian apps): https://streamradar.bricksgenius.com
- **YouTube** (search and downloads): through NewPipeExtractor. Only download what you have the right to keep.
- **Google Input Tools** (Hindi typing), **Cloudflare** (speed test) and **GitHub** (updates).

Some of these are public feeds without an official agreement; they may change or stop working at any time.

Streaming services', apps' and teams' names and logos belong to their owners, and only identify each one. This project has no affiliation with any of them.
