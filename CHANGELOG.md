# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (8 November 2026)

- **Continue watching:** Home shows films and series only; **Show all** has tabs by app (All, My Movies, Netflix, Prime Video…) and a YouTube tab for longer videos (no shorts), and scrolls past five.
- **Screensaver:** a new Settings section to pick TV Town's own, Aerial Views or any installed screensaver, choose what TV Town's shows (your videos, film pictures, or scenes and clock), and try it now.
- **Home Assistant:** a guide to driving TV Town from Home Assistant or a script ([docs/HOME-ASSISTANT.md](docs/HOME-ASSISTANT.md)).
- Tidier repository: the Mac helper scripts moved to `scripts/mac/`.

## 1.0 (7 November 2026)

The first version of TV Town Launcher, with everything in it:

- **Home:** the full picture of what's new on your apps, a floating glass dock, Continue watching (your drive and your streaming apps, up to 15) and listening, My List, the time, weather and live scores.
- **Search:** films, series, songs, apps and YouTube in one place, on a keyboard made for the remote, or typed or spoken on your phone (a QR code sits beside every keyboard).
- **Surprise Me:** moods for films, series, music and extras, in your languages, on the apps you have. Customize every card and add your own.
- **My Movies:** big covers with name, year and rating and a small preview; films and series from a drive matched to their details; tags, favourites, filter, sort and search; resume in VLC; subtitles on request.
- **My Music:** folders, artists, favourites and search; a player that keeps going on Home, with karaoke lyrics, music effects, background videos and a sleep timer.
- **Discover and Must-Watch:** big covers on shelves for every app and mood; the best rated and award-winning titles with IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores; Watched, Want to Watch and ratings.
- **Control Center:** what's playing, sleep timer, speed test, sound, free memory, sports and Turn off.
- **Sports:** cricket, football, basketball, tennis and F1 as you choose; a sports home with live, today's and results in team colours and badges; live scorecards and match stats beside the rows; news with photos and league tables; the live score in the top bar.
- **Phone remote:** frosted glass, every tab on one screen; voice search inside the app on the TV; your apps as big tiles; Remote, Browse, Files and a sideways **game controller** (stick, D-pad, A B X Y, shoulders, Start and Select); downloads and sharing with the TV's drive both ways.
- **Downloads:** audio or video from what's playing (it keeps playing), a link or a search, in the background; OK pressed twice saves from YouTube on any remote.
- **Voice and remote:** the mic opens apps, picks and titles; coloured buttons open My Movies, My Music and Random.
- **Settings:** a small glass card with TV settings at the top, content style, country and languages, backup and restore, updates.
- **Screensaver:** your own videos or film pictures, with a drifting clock, dimmed at night.
- **Home helper:** keeps TV Town the home without turning anything off, repairs itself, and steps aside on request.
- **Private and light:** no ads, accounts or tracking; HTTPS only; smooth on a 1 GB TV; Android 9 and later.
