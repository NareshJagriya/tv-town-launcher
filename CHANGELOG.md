# Changelog

## 1.3 (13 November 2026)

### What's new

- **Home top picture, your choice:** Home's big picture can show New this week, Popular, Top rated, Hidden gems, a genre, a random pick, or your own Watchlist.
- **Continue watching:** Press OK on a title for its details and labelled buttons: Resume, Start over, More info, Mark watched, Remove from row and Fix picture. Titles without a cover get one from TMDB or JustWatch, or a card in the app's colours.
- **Phone remote, redesigned:** Dark and light looks, tabs on top, one-tap TV settings, a full All apps grid, and Send to TV / Get from TV side by side.
- **Your own top bar:** Choose the buttons at the top left and right: inputs, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, screensaver, memory, Control Center, sports and more.
- **Icon packs:** Use an installed icon pack for your apps.
- **Receive with LocalSend:** Send songs and videos from any phone or computer with the free LocalSend app. They're saved on your USB drive.
- **New screensavers:** NASA's image library and Wikimedia's Picture of the Day.
- **Manage your files:** Rename, move, copy or delete films and songs. Their subtitles and lyrics go with them.
- **Surprise Me:** Names on every cover, as many of your own cards as you like, and "+ Add" for your own topics in Extras.
- **Saving from YouTube is safer:** Pressing OK twice now asks first. Choose MP3 or Video, then Download or Cancel, so accidental presses no longer save songs.
- **Sports, full scorecard:** OK on a match opens its full page at once. For cricket that means every innings: every batter with how they got out, every bowler, extras, and who didn't bat.
- **Music player, tidied:** Down opens the controls. Options are in three tabs: Lyrics, Look and Song. My Music shows ♫ or ❚❚ with what's playing.

### Removed

- Subtitle downloads.

### Bug fixes

- Titles open where they should: Netflix, Prime Video and JioHotstar now open straight on the film's or series' page. Netflix used to open on its home page.
- In Spanish, the top picture and some Discover rows came up empty.
- The screensaver list showed "Clock" twice.

## 1.2 (10 November 2026)

### What's new

- **Spanish:** TV Town in Español, chosen in Customize → Language. Film titles, stories and posters follow too, where TMDB has them in Spanish.
- **Stremio and Nuvio:** Choose "Play films in" to open Watch now, Surprise Me and Discover picks straight on the title's page in Stremio or Nuvio. A title that isn't on your streaming apps opens there too.
- **Surprise Me, redesigned:** Full screen like Home, with big mood pictures, tabs in one glass bar, languages as chips and full-screen picks with tall covers.
- **Extras, rethought:** 12 tiles, adding Short Films, Recipes, Podcasts & Talks and Spirituality, with Documentaries reworked. Recipes, Podcasts & Talks, Spirituality and Documentaries let you tick what you like first (diet, cuisine, topic…) or add your own, with choices for India or the rest of the world. Sports Highlights is gone.
- **Home shelf:** Press Down for Continue watching and My Watchlist as tall covers over the picture. Hold OK on a Continue title to mark it watched or remove it.
- **Because you watched:** A new first row in Discover, based on what you've marked Watched.
- **Switch off what you don't use:** Customize → Features can now hide Surprise Me, My Movies and My Music. Live scores moved there too.

### More improvements

- **Faster start:** Home shows its films at once instead of after a few seconds, and keeps working when TMDB is slow.
- **Smoother and sharper:** 60 frames a second on Home where the TV keeps up, and full colour in large pictures.
- **A cleaner look:** One focus style for every cover, smoked glass on the dock, a new Discover sidebar, and text that stays readable on bright pictures.
- **Easier choices:** Settings with more than two options open a list.
- **Update notice:** A dot on Customize and Updates when a new version is out, and What's new before you install it.

### Bug fixes

- The Home button now works from every TV Town screen and pop-up, and no longer flashes Google's home first.
- The empty shelf no longer draws over the film's title and buttons.
- Search no longer labels a title "streaming" when it isn't.
- Two Surprise Me moods no longer share the same picture.

## 1.1 (9 November 2026)

### What's new

- **Speed Up Your TV:** Close background apps and switch off safe-to-disable extras to free memory. Choose what to switch off, turn on Lite mode or undo everything, or press Speed it all up to do it all at once and see how much memory was freed.
- **Continue Watching:** Progress bars on every cover, a quick peek at each app's titles from the dock, and a smaller See all that only appears when there's more to see.
- **Discover, redesigned:** Browse New, Trending, Top rated and Hidden gems, plus more genres including Anime, K-Drama and genre mixes, with a row for each of your apps.
- **Surprise Me, redesigned:** Bright cover art for every choice, 9 Extras per style, English and Hindi switches, more languages, Skip watched and a dice button. Results open in a pop-up.
- **Search everywhere:** A new search bar on Home, Discover, My Music and My Movies, plus "Search in…" for apps that support it. Narrow the results to All, Your drive, Streaming, Music or Apps.
- **Keyboard, redesigned:** A cleaner layout made for the remote.

### More improvements

- **A fresh look:** Shiny black pages and pop-ups, clearer focus, jewel-colour main buttons and green switches across the app.
- **Control Center:** 8 tiles, the most-used first, with Storage and other TV settings moved in from Customize.
- **Lite mode:** Lighter images, no animations, and soft pale colours instead of picture backgrounds.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed a freeze when pressing Back in My Music.
- Fixed the speed test showing "Couldn't test".
- My Music no longer lists the same song twice.

## 1.0 (7 November 2026)

The first version of TV Town Launcher, with everything in it:

- **Home:** the full picture of what's new on your apps, a floating glass dock, Continue watching (your drive and your streaming apps, up to 15) and listening, My List, the time, weather and live scores.
- **Search:** films, series, songs, apps and YouTube in one place, on a keyboard made for the remote, or typed or spoken on your phone (a QR code sits beside every keyboard).
- **Surprise Me:** moods for films, series, music and extras, in your languages, on the apps you have. Customize every card and add your own.
- **My Movies:** big covers with name, year and rating and a small preview; films and series from a drive matched to their details; tags, favourites, filter, sort and search; resume in VLC; subtitles on request.
- **My Music:** folders, artists, favourites and search; a player that keeps going on Home, with karaoke lyrics, music effects, background videos and a sleep timer.
- **Discover and Must-Watch:** big covers on shelves for every app and mood; the best rated and award-winning titles with IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores; Watched, Want to Watch and ratings.
- **Control Center:** what's playing, sleep timer, speed test, sound, free memory, sports and Turn off.
- **Sports:** cricket, football, basketball, tennis and F1 as you choose; a sports home with live, today's and results in team colours and badges; live scorecards and match stats beside the rows; news with photos and league tables; the live score in the top bar.
- **Phone remote:** frosted glass, every tab on one screen; voice search inside the app on the TV; your apps as big tiles; Remote, Browse, Files and a sideways **game controller** (stick, D-pad, A B X Y, shoulders, Start and Select); downloads and sharing with the TV's drive both ways.
- **Downloads:** audio or video from what's playing (it keeps playing), a link or a search, in the background; OK pressed twice saves from YouTube on any remote.
- **Voice and remote:** the mic opens apps, picks and titles; coloured buttons open My Movies, My Music and Random.
- **Settings:** a small glass card with TV settings at the top, content style, country and languages, backup and restore, updates.
- **Screensaver:** your own videos or film pictures, with a drifting clock, dimmed at night.
- **Home helper:** keeps TV Town the home without turning anything off, repairs itself, and steps aside on request.
- **Private and light:** no ads, accounts or tracking; HTTPS only; smooth on a 1 GB TV; Android 9 and later.
