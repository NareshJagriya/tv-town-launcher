# Privacy

TV Town Launcher has **no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking**. It sends no personal data to its author or
anyone else.

## What stays on your TV (or your drive)
Your settings, apps order, watched list and ratings, favourites, tags, resume points, recently played songs, and any
keys you add. Backups are a file you save yourself, on your drive or in Downloads.

## What TV Town asks the internet for
Only what a screen needs, over HTTPS:
- **TMDB, JustWatch, Wikidata:** films and series, artwork, where they stream, links to open them.
- **YouTube's public pages:** songs, videos and trailers for Surprise Me and search.
- **LRCLIB, Apple's iTunes Search, Wikipedia:** lyrics, song details and covers, singers' photos.
- **OpenSubtitles:** subtitles, only when you ask, with your own key.
- **Open-Meteo:** the weather for the town you set.
- **GitHub:** once a day, whether a newer version is out.

These services see the request (like any website you visit) but not who you are.

## Permissions, and why
- **Accessibility (TV Town home helper):** sees only the Home button and which app is in front, to bring TV Town back
  as your home. It never reads what is on the screen.
- **Notification access:** to show what's playing and Continue Watching from your apps. TV Town reads no notifications.
- **Microphone:** only while you hold the mic button on TV Town, through Google's voice input.
- **Files and media:** to find films and songs on your drive, and save subtitles and lyrics next to them.
- **Install apps:** only when you choose Settings → Updates.
